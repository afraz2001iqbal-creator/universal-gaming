Why "Universal"?

Most channels pick one game and stay there. Universal Gaming exists because I never could — every world has something worth learning, and every genre teaches a different way to play.

From Roblox's endless sandbox to the split-second calls of Free Fire, the build battles of Fortnite, and the tactical firefights of Bloodstrike — I explore it all and bring back the best of every universe.

If you're the kind of gamer who's curious about everything, not just one lobby — you're in the right place.
